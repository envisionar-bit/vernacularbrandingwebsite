01 — Order

What Happens Next

Your order for Vernacular Branding has been successfully processed and recorded in our archive.

Confirmation

A receipt with your order details is sent to the email address you used at checkout. If you can’t find it, please check your spam folder.

Fulfilment — India

If you chose standard delivery, a Shiprocket tracking number is activated as soon as the book ships. For in-store pickup, look out for our studio email with collection details. Delivery takes 3–5 days.

Fulfilment — Germany

Your book is dispatched from Germany with tracked shipping; tracking details are provided upon shipment. Delivery takes 3–5 working days.

About the Project

The Vernacular Branding project grew through many voices, images, and perspectives.

In September 2025, we opened an Open Call for Contributions, inviting artists, designers, and photographers to share works that reflect India’s visual and cultural identity.

Over twenty days, we received submissions from across the country — from homes, studios, and streets — each offering a fragment of how design lives in everyday life.

This book is built on those fragments.
To every contributor — thank you for helping us see India anew.

To everyone who shared their work, lent their personal stories, or offered vital encouragement along the way—thank you.
Your contributions have shaped the foundation of Vernacular Branding and remain integral to its continuing journey.

This digital directory acts as a living canvas, reflecting a project that continues to grow with every street documentation, field snapshot, and collaborative dialogue. We want to ensure that every voice, practitioner, and researcher who actively expands this discourse is beautifully recognized.

03 — Press Kit

The Story

Vernacular Branding is an intergenerational collaboration between Prof. Bhuleshwar Mate and Kaushambi Mate. It bridges fifty years of Indian design education with contemporary European architectural research.

While global branding moves toward uniformity, India’s vernacular landscape remains rooted in local knowledge and manual skill. This book serves as a living archive of a disappearing culture, treating the streets as galleries and everyday signs as cultural heritage.

04 — Press Kit

Quick Facts

Authors: Prof. Bhuleshwar Mate & Kaushambi Mate.
Focus: Vernacular typography, hand-painted signage, and informal branding in India.
Recognition: Endorsed by the College of Fine Arts (Thiruvananthapuram), the South Central Zone Cultural Center (SCZCC), the National Institute of Fashion Technology (Hyderabad), and many others.
Launch: Formally launched at the Government College of Art and Design (GCAD), Nagpur, on its 50th anniversary.

05 — Press Kit

Media Highlights & Reception

The project has gained significant attention for its role in preserving the visual language of the Indian street.
Institutional Praise: “A timely intervention in the discourse on visual culture and design practices.” — Prof. Manoj Kannan, Principal, College of Fine Arts, Thiruvananthapuram.
Press Coverage: Featured in Dainik Bhaskar, Lokmat Times, Navbharat Times, Lokshahi Varta, and more.
Broadcast: Featured on Shankhnaad News for its contribution to documenting regional identity.

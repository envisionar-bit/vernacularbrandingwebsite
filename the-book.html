The Book

Vernacular Branding: Reading Identity Through Design and Visibility

A seminal visual research publication exploring everyday identity, grassroots design, and visual culture in India.

This beautifully crafted hardcover edition brings together extensive fieldwork, archival documentation, and insightful essays into a richly illustrated, tactile object.
